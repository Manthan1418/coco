A tiny friend that lives in your notch

Coucou watches your Claude Code sessions, lets you approve its requests from the notch, and keeps an eye on Stripe, GitHub, Vercel, n8n and more. Free and open source.

Download for MacDownload for WindowsView on GitHub
Private by design. No account, no analytics, no tracking. Your keys stay in your Keychain, or in Windows Credential Manager. Read the privacy policy.

Requires macOS 15 or later, or Windows 10/11. On Windows the installer isn’t code-signed yet, so SmartScreen shows a warning — click More info → Run anyway.
